How RKS is calculated

For ACC ≥ 70%:

single RKS = constant × ((ACC − 55) / 45)²

For ACC < 70%, single RKS is 0. Your final RKS is B27 + P3: the 27 highest single-RKS charts plus the 3 highest-constant charts you Phi'd. A chart can appear in both.
